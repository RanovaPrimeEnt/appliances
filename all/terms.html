TERMS OF USE

Terms for using the RPE product catalogue.

Terms for using the Ranova Prime Enterprise online product catalogue and enquiry service.

Important: catalogue content is provided for browsing and enquiry. A displayed product is not a confirmed offer of stock or a final price until RPE confirms it directly.

Catalogue information

RPE aims to present product names, descriptions and images clearly. Product colour, packaging, model details or specifications may change. Customers should confirm important product details before purchase.

Prices and availability

Where the site says “Ask for price” or “Confirm availability,” the current price and stock status must be confirmed directly with RPE.

Orders

Adding products to a selection does not place or pay for an order. A purchase is completed only after RPE and the customer confirm the relevant order details through an agreed communication channel.

Payments

The current website does not store payment-card information or operate an online checkout. Do not enter card details into any field on this catalogue.

Website use

Do not misuse the website, interfere with its operation or attempt to use the catalogue content in a misleading way. These terms may be updated as RPE adds new features.
