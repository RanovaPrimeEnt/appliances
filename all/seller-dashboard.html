Seller wallet & payouts

Your sales money moves through protected stages. A customer payment does not become withdrawable until RANOVA's delivery, hold, refund, dispute, after-sales and safety checks pass.

PendingGHS 0.00

Pending = customer paid but protection conditions are not finished. Available = eligible for Owner-controlled payout. Held = refund/dispute/after-sales/safety review. Processing = transfer sent to provider. Paid = provider confirmed transfer success.
RANOVA stores the payment-provider recipient code and only a masked destination, not the full bank/MoMo number. Commission is calculated on product subtotal; delivery is excluded from commission.
