ORDER POLICIES

Shipping, returns and refunds — explained clearly.

Read how Ranova Prime Enterprise currently handles order confirmation, delivery, returns, refunds and cancellations.

Current ordering model: this website is a catalogue and enquiry service. It does not currently collect card payments or automatically confirm orders.

Order confirmation

Products shown on the catalogue are subject to confirmation. RPE confirms the current price, availability, quantity, delivery arrangement and any applicable warranty information directly with the customer before a purchase is completed.

Shipping and delivery

Delivery charges, delivery area and estimated timing are confirmed during the order conversation. They are not automatically calculated on this website.

Returns

If you receive an incorrect, damaged or defective item, contact RPE promptly using the phone, WhatsApp or email details on this site. Do not return an item before RPE confirms the return arrangement.

Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on the specific order, the condition of the product and the terms confirmed with the customer before purchase. Because the website does not take online payments, refunds are not processed through this website.

Cancellations

If you need to cancel or change an enquiry, contact RPE before the order is finalized or dispatched. Once dispatch has begun, any change may depend on the delivery arrangement already agreed.
