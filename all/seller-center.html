Your verification checklist

Do one step at a time. Choose the requested file, upload it, then move to the next step. RANOVA Admin will review the real document securely.

Before uploading: use clear PDF, Word (DOC/DOCX), PowerPoint (PPT/PPTX), or image files, make sure names/numbers are readable, do not crop important details, and only submit documents that genuinely belong to the business or its authorized representative. Maximum file size: 10 MB each.

STEP 3 · REQUIREDNot uploaded Verify your identity

What to upload: a valid government-issued ID for the person responsible for the seller account. Make sure the name, photo and important details are clear.

OPTIONAL SUPPORTOptional Add business location proof

Optional: utility bill, tenancy/lease document, shop or warehouse evidence, or another genuine document supporting the business location.

What to upload: genuine stock/shop/warehouse photos, inventory or supplier evidence, delivery/courier arrangement, or other proof that you can prepare and deliver customer orders.
