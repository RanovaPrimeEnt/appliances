What happens after applying?

  1. RANOVA receives your application.
  2. Your business information is reviewed.
  3. If eligible, you continue to verification.
  4. You set up your store and products.
  5. RANOVA approves the store before it goes public.
Important: applying does not automatically make a business a verified seller. Verification status and badges will only appear after the required checks are completed.
