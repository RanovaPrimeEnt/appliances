RANOVA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Ranova Prime Enterprise operates RANOVA, a marketplace connecting customers and sellers. We collect information needed to create and secure accounts, operate stores, process orders and payments, provide delivery and support, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations.

Information we process

This can include account and contact details, seller verification documents, store and product information, orders, delivery addresses, messages, payment references and transaction status, device/technical information, and files or media you choose to upload.

How we use information

We use information to provide RANOVA, authenticate users, operate marketplace and seller features, process and reconcile transactions, communicate order and account updates, provide support, improve reliability and security, and comply with applicable law.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers required to operate the platform, including hosting/database, payment and delivery providers, and with sellers or buyers where necessary to fulfil marketplace transactions. We do not sell personal information.

Security and retention

We use technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service. Some transaction records may need to be retained after an account is closed for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute requirements.

Your choices

You may update account details in RANOVA and may request account deletion. See Account Deletion.
